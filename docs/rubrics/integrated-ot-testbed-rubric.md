# Integrated OT Testbed Rubric — 100 Marks

| Criterion | Excellent standard | Marks |
|---|---|---:|
| Isolated architecture and functional plant baseline | Clear, original topology; working process; correct isolation and documented normal state | 20 |
| Modbus/TCP implementation | Accurate data model, register map, units/ranges and explained normal client/server behaviour | 15 |
| Visibility and exposure assessment | Strong packet analysis, appropriately scoped discovery and evidence-based risk record | 15 |
| Defensive OT guard/control | Meaningful validation logic, observable decisions, structured logs and clear limitations | 20 |
| Controlled validation and recovery | Planned authorised scenarios, credible comparison, safe containment and verified restoration | 15 |
| Report, evidence and defence | Complete professional report, traceable evidence, original work and confident technical explanation | 15 |
| **Total** |  | **100** |

## Evidence quality standard

For every claim, identify the associated packet, log, screenshot, test result or system-state observation. The assessor must be able to trace your conclusion back to an evidence file.

## Academic integrity standard

Marks may be withheld where code, captures, screenshots, reports or records are copied, fabricated, incomplete or cannot be explained by the learner/team during defence.
