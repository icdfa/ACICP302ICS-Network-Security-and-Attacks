# Integrated Source-Code-Guided OT Lab Rubric — 100 Marks

| Criterion | Excellent standard | Marks |
|---|---|---:|
| Safe source-code lab operation, architecture and baseline | Correctly identifies the canonical source and commit; follows VM isolation/safety; clearly explains component roles, data flow and normal process state using observed evidence | 20 |
| Modbus/TCP data model and normal-operation analysis | Accurate source-based register map, units/ranges and alarm/state meaning; clear explanation of normal client/server and read behaviour | 15 |
| Packet visibility and scoped exposure analysis | Usable learner-created capture; accurate request/response interpretation linked to process state; exposure inventory remains strictly within documented loopback scope; evidence-based risk record | 15 |
| Supplied guard analysis and audit evidence | Correctly explains implemented safeguards and decisions; uses relevant structured events/logs; identifies assumptions and limitations without overstating protection | 20 |
| Controlled validation and recovery | At least three approved bounded scenarios; clear expected/observed comparison; evidence supports conclusions; safe shutdown and recovery are verified | 15 |
| Report, evidence and defence | Complete professional report and traceable evidence index; source is attributed; original analysis is coherent and the learner/team can explain it | 15 |
| **Total** |  | **100** |

## Evidence quality standard

For every material claim, identify the supporting packet, log, screenshot, test result or system-state observation. State the source-code path and commit used. The assessor must be able to trace conclusions to evidence from the learner’s assigned simulation.

## Academic integrity standard

Use of the instructor-provided source code is authorized and expected. Do not claim that source as your own. Marks may be withheld where analysis or evidence is copied, fabricated, incomplete, unsafe, or cannot be explained by the learner/team during the defence.
